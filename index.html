Clip Rush TV

Clip Rush TV is a YouTube channel with short clips of the funniest and craziest GTA 6 and streamer moments. Every clip credits the streamer and links to their channel.

Clipper is the channel's own private tool: it prepares the clips and, when the channel owner approves one, uploads it to the Clip Rush TV YouTube channel and reads the channel's statistics.
